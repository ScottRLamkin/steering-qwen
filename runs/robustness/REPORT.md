# Does surviving quantization measure a neuron's meaning?

Panel: 95 neurons over 19 traits (hedwig: 9, new: 10), plus 20 random neurons. 75 neurons have bf16 specificity above 0 (bootstrap 95% interval over 10 held-out questions). Formats: bf16, NF4, int8, FP4, fake int4, fake int3.

Definitions are in `src/steering_llms/neurons/robustness_report.py`. Retention R = specificity in the format / specificity in bf16, for the significant neurons; the edit is the same `down_proj`-input value in every format.
